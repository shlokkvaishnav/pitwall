Formula 1 race-strategy engine

Lap-time, safety-car and overtaking models, combined with a Monte Carlo simulation of the rest of the race to evaluate pit-stop strategies. Public lap data only, running in your browser.

  • About 200x faster batch simulator than the one-race-at-a-time one (three races, measured under other load)

  • 17% lower lap-time error than "next lap = last lap", on six unseen 2026 races

What did not work

  • The pit-window check lost to a fixed rule. The engine's 7-lap pit window holds the top 5 finishers' real stop in 29% of 591 stops (2023 to 2025), below a fixed-lap rule (37%).
  • The simulated regret check is SIMULATED. It is scored by the engine's own model, so it is not independent evidence: the engine's laps cost 2.3 s less simulated race time than the teams' real laps, and the same as the fixed-lap rule.
  • The full-race simulators were no better than the starting grid at forecasting the finishing order.
