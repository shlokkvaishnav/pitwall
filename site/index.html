pitwall

This project finds the hidden battery state and tyre wear of 2026 F1 cars from public timing data. Then it uses them in a race simulator and a pit-strategy agent.

Status: M0 is done. The charter, the holdout lock and the energy store are ready. There are no results yet.

How it works

  1. A state-space model estimates the battery charge and the tyre wear of each car.
  2. A race simulator uses these states. We compare it with real races first.
  3. A strategy agent learns pit decisions in the simulator, only if the simulator passes its test.

Tests

HoldoutThe 2026 races on or after 1 September 2026. They are locked until the final spec.
Lap timeThe error must be at least 5% lower than the best baseline (Diebold-Mariano p < 0.05).
StraightsThe error of the speed loss at the end of long straights must be at least 10% lower.
SimulatorThe finishing order must be better than the grid order.
This site shows statistics only. Data: FastF1 and Jolpica-F1. FastF1 data is not official F1 data.
